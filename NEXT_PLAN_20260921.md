## STEP 1 - Fix the throughput defect (P0, blocks the demo)

### The defect
`rush_50`, fleet 50, 900 ticks, seeds 11/13/17, identical seed per arm:

| arm | s11 | s13 | s17 | TOTAL | collisions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline (stop-and-wait) | 9 | 3 | 10 | **22** | 0/0/0 |
| swarmos (full) | 5 | 3 | 3 | **11** | 1/3/0 |

SWARMOS loses `tasks_per_min` - the dashboard hero metric - to the
strawman it exists to beat.

### What is already ruled out (do NOT retry)
Four pairwise-geometry fixes inside `_monitor`, all measured, all reverted:
1. non-degradation floor -> 11 completions (unchanged) AND +1/+3 collisions
2. `MONITOR_STUCK_TICKS` sweep 4/8/16/40 -> 6/12/8/6, non-monotonic
3. `HARD_STOP_M` sweep 0.75/0.70/0.60/0.50 -> 6/10/7/6, collisions 1/19/264/262
4. contest-at-floor -> reverted

Decisive evidence that the floor is not the cause: vetoes stayed
~10k-13k while the floor shrank by a third. And the fleet curve
(8: 10 v 11, 16: 16 v 19, 24: 21 v 29, 50: 6 v 22) shows congestion
collapse between 24 and 50 - a global effect, not a constant.

### The fix to try
A held robot's held-ness is invisible to the robots queued behind it, so
they replan into the same blocked space. Halt RATES are near-identical
between arms (12.58 vs 12.47 per tick); the difference is 2.2x the
replan churn.

`PeerView.holding` already exists and is already broadcast. `_monitor`
consumes `granted`; `_plan()` (`app/sim/engine.py` L738) does not - a held
robot looks navigable to the planner. Make the planner treat the cell
occupied by a held peer as blocked.

Why this is safe to attempt: it only ever REMOVES candidate paths, so it
costs no part of the Simplex safety argument. It is a global fix to a
global problem.

### Procedure (non-negotiable)
- `cp app/coordination/swarm_policy.py /tmp/swarm_policy.before_planner.py`
  FIRST - the file is untracked by git, there is no revert path.
- Every patch script gates on `ast.parse` before writing, and asserts
  `src.count(old) == 1` before each replace.
- Measure seeds 11/13/17 at 900 ticks, fleet 50.
- **Target: beat 22 completions with collisions at 0.**
- Back out immediately if collisions appear at any seed.

### Fallback if it does not land (STEP 1b)
Throttle concurrent active tasks by density. `tasks_active` was pinned at
exactly 50 of 50 in every fleet-50 run - the dispatcher never throttles.
Gate `_dispatch()` (`engine.py` L626) on a congestion measure.

### Last resort (STEP 1c)
Demo at fleet 24, where SWARMOS is collision-free and near parity
(21 v 29). Honest and defensible. Fleet 50 is not.
